How we help

Most clients come to us with one question and leave with a complete plan. Here is where we spend most of our time.

Retirement income

When you can retire and where each paycheque will come from: pensions, RRIFs, TFSAs and government benefits.

Investment planning

Diversified portfolios built around your goals, with plain-language reviews of what changed and why.

Insurance and protection

Life, disability and critical illness coverage sized to your family and your mortgage.

Families and education

RESPs, TFSAs and first-home savings for young families getting started.

Business owners

Paying yourself, group benefits for your staff and a plan for the day you sell or step back.

Estate conversations

Wills, beneficiaries and powers of attorney, coordinated with your lawyer and accountant.

Our approach

A plan you can explain to your family

We start with what you want your money to do, not with products. Every client gets a written plan in plain language, one lead advisor who knows their story, and a team of specialists behind them.

  • One written plan that covers investments, insurance and retirement together
  • A yearly review, plus a call whenever life changes
  • Clear fees, explained before you sign anything
