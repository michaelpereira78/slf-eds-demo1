Roles

Who can do what

Advisors look after their own pages. Supervisors decide what goes live. The platform team looks after the setup. These rules are enforced, not just hidden in the toolbar.

Advisor

Edit, preview, delete drafts and request reviews. Cannot publish or change site settings.

Supervisor

Everything an advisor can do, plus publish, unpublish, review locked versions and publish many pages at once.

Platform admin

Site setup, roles, new sites and connections. The platform team and the owner.

Roles are set once in the site’s access configuration and checked on every publish. The /admin and /advisor pages themselves are ordinary pages; the real control is who is allowed to publish.
