Approvals

Review what advisors have sent

Approve a page or send it back with a short note. Each decision is written to the audit log. When a page is approved, you publish it and the live site updates.

Review queue

How to publish

  1. Open the approved page’s preview (Open preview in the queue).
  2. Click Publish in the toolbar. It is only available on a supervisor account; advisors see it unavailable.
  3. The live site updates worldwide in a few seconds, and a Published row is added to the audit log.

Publishing many pages at once, for example a new site launch: select the documents in the folder and use bulk Publish.

This is enforced by the platform: a publish from an account that is not a supervisor is rejected, whether it comes from the toolbar, a script or a bookmark.
