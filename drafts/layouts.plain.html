Capability 3: layout changes

Layout gallery

Each band below is the same kind of content with a different style. To change one, edit the Style cell in that section’s Section Metadata table, then Preview. No developer needed.

Default

No Section Metadata. White background, left-aligned text, full width.

Style: Light

A soft tinted band. Good for team lists and contact details.

Style: Dark

The brand colour as a band, for a statement or call to action.

A button on a dark band

Style: Accent

A warm accent band that follows the site’s theme.

Style: Centered

Centred headings and text, for short messages and campaign pages.

Style: Dark, Centered

Styles combine: separate them with a comma.

Book a meeting

Style: Narrow

A reading width of about 70 characters for long articles and bios. Long lines are harder to read, so this style keeps them short.

Style: Sidebar

On a desktop, the last block in the section (here, a contact card) moves to a sticky column on the right. On a phone, it stacks underneath.

Use it for advisor profiles and service pages.

Block variants

Blocks change look with a word in brackets after the block name: Hero, Hero (Light), Hero (Centered), Hero (Profile); Contact or Contact (Card); Callout or Callout (CTA); Cards or Cards (Capabilities).

Columns

Put content side by side by adding cells to a Columns table.

Two or three

On phones, columns stack automatically.

Any content

Text, images, lists and buttons all work inside columns.
