Help and FAQ

You can

  • Edit your own pages in Google Docs or Word
  • Preview changes on your private preview address
  • Use approved content and approved templates
  • Change section styles and block variants
  • Submit pages for compliance review

Your supervisor does

  • Approves your changes with Compliance
  • Publishes approved pages to your live site
  • Makes advanced layout, theme and template changes
  • Creates new advisor sites and Figma imports

Publish is greyed out for advisors in Sidekick. This is enforced by the platform’s role settings, not just hidden: an advisor account has the author role, which cannot publish.

Submit for compliance review

Finished a change? Tell Compliance what changed. The page joins the review queue and the submission is written to the compliance audit log (date, advisor, site, page, summary).

How to update your site

Open the doc

In Google Drive, open your site folder and the page’s document (index is your home page).

Make the change

Edit text, swap a photo, or paste approved content from Sidekick > Library.

Preview

Click Preview in Sidekick. Your private preview updates in about three seconds.

Submit for review

Click Request compliance review and say what changed.

Supervisor publishes

After approval your supervisor publishes, and the live site updates worldwide.

Help and FAQ

Why can’t I publish?

Advisor accounts have the author role: edit and preview only. Publishing is reserved for supervisors so that only Compliance-approved content reaches the live site.

How do I change my photo?

In the Team Cards table, click the photo in the first column, replace it with your new picture (Insert > Image), then Preview.

How do I add approved product wording?

Sidekick > Library > Approved content > Copy link, then paste the link on its own line. Or copy the link from the approved content page.

Approved content library

My change is not showing on the preview

Wait five seconds and click Preview again; Google Docs saves in the background. Still stuck? Check you edited the document in your own site folder.

Who do I contact?

Your supervisor for content and layout questions; MarTech Engineering (martech-engineering@example.com) for anything technical.
