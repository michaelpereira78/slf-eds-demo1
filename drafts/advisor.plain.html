Help and FAQ

How an update goes live

Open the page

In your site folder, open the document for the page you want to change. The home page is called index.

Make the change

Edit the text, replace a photo, or paste a piece of approved wording.

Preview and send

Preview is private, and ready in a few seconds. Then send a short note so Compliance can take a look.

It goes live

Once it is approved, your supervisor publishes it and your live website updates worldwide.

You can

  • Edit your own pages in a document
  • Preview changes on a private address
  • Use approved wording and approved templates
  • Switch a section style yourself
  • Send pages for compliance review

Your supervisor does

  • Reviews your changes with Compliance
  • Publishes approved pages to your live site
  • Handles bigger layout and brand changes
  • Sets up new advisor websites

You will notice the Publish button is unavailable on your account. That is by design: only a supervisor can put a page live, so nothing reaches the public site before it is approved.
