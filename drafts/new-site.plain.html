Capability 1: new websites

Request a new advisor site

Answer five questions. Pick a theme and watch this page change to match. The platform team generates the site’s starter documents from your answers.

Form

What happens after you submit

  1. The platform team runs the site generator with your answers. It creates the home page, navigation and footer documents, already set to your theme and template.
  2. They create a Drive (or SharePoint) folder for the site, share it with you, and upload the documents.
  3. They create the site with one call to the platform’s admin service. No new code, no new hosting.
  4. You edit the documents, Preview, request a compliance review, and Compliance publishes.

Examples generated this way: Harbourview Financial Group and Northstar Planning.
