Advisor web platform demo

Seven things business users can do without a developer

Everything on this demo is authored in ordinary documents and runs on Adobe Edge Delivery Services. All firms, people and contact details are fictional.

Two workspaces, two roles

Advisors maintain their own sites; supervisors approve, publish and grow the platform.

Advisor workspace

Edit and preview your site, use approved content and templates, request layout changes, submit for compliance review and track your submissions. Author role: no publishing.

Open the advisor workspace

Supervisor console

Approve submissions, publish, create new advisor sites from templates, make advanced layout updates and import custom designs from Figma. Publish role.

Open the supervisor console

The seven capabilities

1. Generate a new website

Fill in a short request form and pick a theme and template. The platform team turns it into a ready-to-edit folder of documents in minutes.

Open the site request form

2. Make content changes

Advisors edit the Word or Google Doc and click Preview. After approval a supervisor publishes, and it is live worldwide in seconds.

Open the advisor workspace

3. Make minor layout changes

Change a section style (Light, Dark, Centered, Sidebar) or a block variant by typing one word in the document.

See the layout gallery

4. Use approved templates

Start every page from an approved template: Advisor team, Solo advisor or Campaign. Developers can add new ones without touching existing sites.

Browse approved templates

5. Apply a brand theme

Each site picks a theme with one metadata value. Same platform, different look.

See Harbourview (Harbour theme)

6. Reuse approved content

Product descriptions, calls to action and disclosures are written once by Compliance and reused everywhere.

Open the approved content library

7. Compliance review and approval

Advisors submit for review; supervisors approve or send back changes and publish. Every step is logged in an audit sheet.

Open the supervisor console
