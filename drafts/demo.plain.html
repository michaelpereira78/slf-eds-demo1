Advisor web platform demo

Two workspaces. One platform. No developer required.

Advisors update their own websites in ordinary documents. Supervisors review, approve and launch new sites. Everything you will see is fictional, and this is not a Sun Life website.

Two workspaces, two roles

Each role has its own home, its own menu, and only the tools that belong there.

Advisor workspace

A simple home for advisors: update a page, add a page, use approved wording, send a change for review and follow its progress. Advisors cannot publish.

Open the advisor workspace

Supervisor console

A dashboard for supervisors: the review queue, advisor sites, new site launches, layout changes, Figma imports and the audit log. Supervisors publish.

Open the supervisor console

What you can do, without a developer

1. Generate a new website

From the supervisor console, pick a template and a look. The starter pages for the new practice are ready to edit in minutes.

Open the new site form

2. Make content changes

Advisors open a document, make a change and preview it privately. After approval a supervisor publishes, and it is live worldwide in seconds.

Open the advisor workspace

3. Make minor layout changes

Change a section style (Light, Dark, Centered, Sidebar) or a block variant by typing one word in the document.

See the layout gallery

4. Use approved templates

Start every page from an approved template: Advisor team, Solo advisor or Campaign. Developers can add new ones without touching existing sites.

Browse approved templates

5. Apply a brand theme

Each site picks a theme with one metadata value. Same platform, different look.

See Harbourview (Harbour theme)

6. Reuse approved content

Product descriptions, calls to action and disclosures are written once by Compliance and reused everywhere.

Open the approved content library

7. Compliance review and approval

Advisors submit a short note from their workspace; supervisors approve or send back changes from the console and then publish. Every step is logged.

Open the approval queue
