Wording you can trust, ready to paste

Product descriptions, invitations and the legal disclosure are written once by Compliance. You place them on your pages; you cannot edit the words, and that is the point: every site stays accurate.

Open the full library

How to add a piece to a page

  1. Open the library and copy the link for the piece you need.
  2. In your page document, paste the link on its own line.
  3. Preview. The approved wording appears, badge and all.

When Compliance updates a piece, every site that uses it picks up the new wording the next time the page is published.
