Edit content

Change a page in a few minutes

You work in an ordinary document, the same way you would edit a letter. Save, preview, and you will see your update on a private copy of the page.

The steps

Open the document

In your site folder, open the document for that page. The home page is the one named index.

Make the change

Edit a heading, replace a photo, or add a sentence. For product wording, paste a piece of approved content instead of writing your own.

Preview

Click Preview in the toolbar on the document. Your private preview updates in about three seconds.

Send for review

When you are happy with it, submit the page so Compliance can take a look.

Submit a change for review

Browse approved wording

Things you can do yourself

  • Change names, titles, phone numbers and short biographies
  • Replace a photo: click the picture, insert a new one, then preview
  • Switch a section’s look by changing one word (for example Light to Dark)
  • Paste approved product wording and the legal disclosure

Bigger changes, such as a new section or a different page layout, can be requested from your supervisor.
