Help

Questions, answered quickly

A short list of the things advisors ask most often. If you are still stuck, your supervisor is the first call.

Why can’t I publish?

Advisor accounts can edit and preview only. Publishing is reserved for supervisors, so only approved content reaches the live site. This is a platform rule, not a hidden button.

How do I change my photo?

In the team table, click the photo in the first column, replace it with your new picture, then preview.

How do I add approved product wording?

Copy the link from the approved content library and paste it on its own line in your page.

Approved content

My change is not showing on the preview

Wait five seconds and click Preview again; documents save in the background. Still stuck? Check you edited the document in your own site folder.

Who do I contact?

Your supervisor for content and layout questions; the platform team (martech-engineering@example.com) for anything technical.
