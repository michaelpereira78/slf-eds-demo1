Then do this

  1. Open the templates folder in your site’s document library and make a copy of the one you want.
  2. Move the copy into your site folder and give it a clear name.
  3. Replace the text in square brackets, add your photo, and click Preview.
  4. Submit the new page for review when you are happy with it.
