6 ways to use your critical illness insurance payout

A critical illness can affect more than your health. It can affect your income, your expenses and how much time you’re able to take away from work.

That’s where critical illness insurance can help. The payout is yours to use based on what you need most.

Why bundle critical illness insurance with life insurance?

• Life insurance helps protect your loved ones when you’re gone.

• Critical illness insurance helps protect you and your loved ones while you’re still here.
