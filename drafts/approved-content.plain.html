Capability 6: approved content

Approved content library

Compliance writes and approves these blocks once. Advisors reuse them by pasting a link; they cannot change the wording. When Compliance updates a block, every site that uses it shows the new version on its next publish.

How advisors use it

  1. In the document, click Sidekick, then Library, then Approved content.
  2. Click Copy link next to the block you need.
  3. Paste it on its own line in your page and click Preview. The approved text appears.

No Sidekick handy? Type the link text shown under each block below on its own line and make it a link. Both ways give the same result.
