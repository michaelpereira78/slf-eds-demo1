About Jordan

Jordan started Maple Ridge Wealth Partners with one idea: financial advice should be easy to understand. Today Jordan leads a team of six and works closely with families, incorporated professionals and small business owners across southern Alberta.

Outside the office, Jordan coaches youth soccer, volunteers with a local food bank and is always up for a weekend hike in Kananaskis.
